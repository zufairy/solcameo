**Read this before you make a clip. It tells you what Mach is, in plain words, so you know what to say.**

## What is an "agent," really?

An **agent** is an AI worker you own. Not a chatbot that just talks — a worker that *does a job and gets paid for it.* There are three main kinds. Pick whichever is easiest for you to explain in a clip:

| Agent type | What it does | The money line you can say |
|---|---|---|
| **AI Influencer** | Makes short videos, posts to TikTok/Reels daily, builds a following, runs brand campaigns | *"It posts 30 videos a month and earns from the views."* |
| **AI Worker** | Answers WhatsApp/Instagram DMs for a business 24/7, recommends products, closes sales | *"It replies in 60 seconds at 3AM and never misses a sale."* |
| **AI Trader** | Researches crypto markets, tracks signals, sends research to subscribers | *"It watches the market all night and reports back."* |

For this campaign, lean on the **Influencer** and **Worker** examples — they're the easiest for viewers to instantly get.

## Why Mach is different (the part that makes people go "wait, what?")

These are your strongest talking points. Use one per clip — don't cram them all in.

1. **You describe it, it builds itself.** No code. One sentence in, a working agent out, in minutes. *(This is the magic — show it.)*
2. **It earns real money.** Not points, not hype — actual payouts, recorded on-chain so it's provable. Grounded examples: *"+$214 overnight," "an AI influencer earning ~$15K/month," "a shop's sales +23%."*
3. **It works while you sleep.** 24/7, never tired, never quits. The "wake up to earnings" angle.
4. **It remembers and gets better.** Unlike a basic chatbot, a Mach agent remembers every customer and every win — so the longer it runs, the more valuable it becomes. It's an *asset that grows,* not a tool you throw away.
5. **You own it.** It's yours. It can even be funded by investors who share its earnings — like owning a tiny business that runs itself.

## Say this / Don't say this

**✅ Do say:**
- "Launch an AI agent that earns money."
- "Describe it, it goes live in minutes."
- "It works 24/7 and gets paid on-chain."
- "It remembers your customers and gets smarter over time."
- Real grounded numbers (the ones above).

**❌ Don't say:**
- "Get rich quick" / "guaranteed returns" / "passive income forever." (False and kills trust.)
- Made-up giant numbers ("$1M in a week").
- "It's a chatbot." (It's a worker that earns — bigger than that.)
- Anything promising specific profits to viewers — agents *can* fail; keep it honest.
- Nothing NSFW, hateful, or misleading about what Mach does.
