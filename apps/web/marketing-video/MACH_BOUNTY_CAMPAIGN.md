You're a creator. You make a short video about Mach (an app where anyone can launch an AI agent that does real work and earns money). You post it on TikTok, Reels, Shorts, or X. People watch it. **Mach pays you based on how many real views you get.**

## 4. The rules that keep it fair (anti-fraud)

We pay for **verified human views**, not numbers on a screen. Here's how we verify, stated plainly so there are no surprises:

- **7-day hold:** views are counted **7 days after you post**, so fake spikes settle and platforms finalize counts.
- **Proof:** submit your post link; we read public view counts via platform data + a screenshot at the 7-day mark. Big claims may need a screen-recording of your analytics.
- **Bot filter:** obviously purchased views, view-bot patterns, and engagement-pod spam are removed before payout. Repeat offenders are banned from the pool.
- **De-duplication:** the same video reposted across your own accounts counts **once** (highest single count).
- **One pool, first-come:** payouts process weekly; when cumulative payouts hit $2,000 the campaign closes. We announce remaining pool weekly so you know how much is left.
- **Mach's call is final** on what's a genuine view — but we err toward paying creators and we publish every payout for transparency.

# PART B — THE FUNNEL

## 9. The big picture (read this first)

The bounty is the **engine**. The funnel is the **road** that engine drives people down. The bounty creates a flood of clips → the clips create views → the views create clicks → the clicks turn into people who **sign up and launch an AI agent**. That last step — *launching an agent* — is the only thing that actually matters. Everything is built to push toward it.

There are really **two funnels stacked on top of each other:**

- **Funnel 1 — The Creator Funnel:** turns creators into a posting army (so we get cheap, massive reach).
- **Funnel 2 — The Viewer Funnel:** turns the people who *watch* those clips into Mach builders (the real goal).

```
  CREATORS  ──post clips──►  VIEWERS  ──click──►  SIGNUPS  ──onboard──►  LAUNCHED AGENT
   (Funnel 1)                         (Funnel 2)                          ★ THE GOAL ★
      ▲                                                                       │
      └──────────── builders who win become creators (the loop) ◄────────────┘
```

The magic: a builder who launches an agent and earns money becomes the **next creator** posting "look what mine made." The funnel feeds itself.

## 11. Funnel 2 — The Viewer Funnel (turn watchers into builders) ★

This is the one that earns Mach its money. A stranger scrolling TikTok becomes someone running an AI agent. Five stages:

### Stage V1 — HOOK (the first 1 second)
- **Where:** TikTok / Reels / Shorts / X feed.
- **What:** the clip's hook stops the thumb — *"POV: you slept, it worked"*, *"I typed one sentence and it earned $214."*
- **Job:** earn the next 3 seconds. Nothing else.
- **Measure:** 3-second hold rate. *(If this is low, the hook is wrong — fix the hook, not the product.)*

### Stage V2 — INTRIGUE (seconds 2–30)
- **What:** the clip shows the magic gap — describe an agent → it works → it earns on-chain — and ends on **"launch yours → mach.city"**.
- **Job:** plant the thought *"wait, I could do this"* and push the click.
- **Measure:** watch-through %, profile visits, link clicks.

### Stage V3 — LANDING (the click → mach.city)
- **Where:** a dedicated **launch landing page** (not the generic homepage). Same lime/charcoal look as the clip so it feels continuous.
- **What the page must do, top to bottom:**
  1. One headline that repeats the clip's promise: *"Launch an AI agent that earns. In minutes."*
  2. A **live "describe it" box** front-and-center — the visitor types an agent idea right there (interactive = sticky).
  3. 10-second proof: the on-chain payout, a grounded number, Aisha/Sara.
  4. One button: **"Launch your agent free →"**. One CTA, no menu maze.
- **Job:** convert curiosity into a sign-up.
- **Measure:** landing→signup conversion %, time-on-page, describe-box usage.

### Stage V4 — SIGN-UP & ONBOARD (the account)
- **What:** fastest possible sign-up (email or wallet). Then a **guided first-run** that carries the idea they typed on the landing page straight into the Studio — so they don't start from a blank screen.
- **Job:** get them to the "aha" — seeing their described agent assemble itself.
- **Measure:** signup completion %, onboarding drop-off points.

### Stage V5 — ACTIVATION = LAUNCH AN AGENT ★ (the goal)
- **What:** they hit **Deploy** and their first agent goes live. Confetti, a "you're live" moment, and a nudge: *"share what you built → tag #MachAgents"* (this is the loop back into Funnel 1).
- **Job:** the single metric the whole campaign is judged on.
- **Measure:** **% of sign-ups who launch an agent** (north-star), time-to-first-launch.

```
V1 HOOK ──► V2 INTRIGUE ──► V3 LANDING ──► V4 SIGNUP ──► V5 LAUNCH ★
 3s hold     watch+click     page→signup    signup done   agent live
   │             │               │              │             │
 fix hook     fix CTA        fix the page   fix onboarding  celebrate
                                                          + ask to share ──► back to Funnel 1
```

## 12. The loop (why this compounds)

A builder who launches an agent and sees it earn is your **best creator** — their content is authentic and converts harder than any ad. So at Stage V5 we explicitly ask them to post their result and **join the bounty** (`mach.city/bounty`). Now they're back at the top of Funnel 1, posting for the next wave. Each cycle should cost less per new builder than the last. That's the flywheel:

> **post → views → clicks → signups → launches → those builders post → more views…**

## 13. Tracking — what we watch and where it can break

| Funnel stage | The one number that matters | If it's low, the problem is… |
|---|---|---|
| Clips posted | total verified views | not enough creators / weak incentive → boost bonuses, push the listing |
| V1 Hook | 3-sec hold rate | the hook → re-cut the first second, test new hooks |
| V2 Intrigue | link-click rate | weak CTA or no clear "why" → sharpen the end card |
| V3 Landing | landing→signup % | the page → simplify, make the describe-box the hero |
| V4 Signup | signup completion % | too much friction → fewer fields, wallet/email one-tap |
| V5 Launch ★ | **signup→launch %** | onboarding → carry their idea in, remove blank-screen |
| Loop | creators who came from V5 | not asking → add the "share what you built" prompt |

**Setup needed before launch:** unique `mach.city/r/` referral links per creator (UTM-tagged), an analytics event on each stage (click → landing → signup → launch), and the bounty dashboard showing views + payouts + leaderboard publicly.

**Honest math for this $2K campaign:** ~666K views at, say, a 1.5% click rate = ~10,000 landing visits. At a 12% signup rate = ~1,200 signups. At a 35% launch rate = **~420 new agents launched** for a $2,000 + $500 spend = roughly **$6 per launched agent.** These are planning estimates to beat, not promises — the whole point of the tracking above is to find which stage is dragging and fix it.

## 15. FAQ

**Do I need a lot of followers?** No. We pay for views, not followers. A 200-follower account with a great hook can out-earn a big one.

**When do I get paid?** Weekly, after each clip clears its 7-day verification, once you pass 5,000 views ($15).

**What if the pool runs out?** The campaign closes and we announce it. We post the remaining balance every week so you can see it coming. First to post, first to earn.

**Can I use AI to make the videos?** Yes — that's encouraged. The 30 scripts are written for Seedance + screen-recording. Just don't use bought/bot views.

**Can I post the same clip on TikTok and Reels?** Yes, post everywhere — but the same video across your own accounts is de-duplicated and counts once (highest single count).

**Why the launch bonus?** Because Mach only wins when people actually launch an agent. Views are great; a launched agent is the goal — so we pay extra for it.
