# Mach.city — Staff Video Handbook

**For the team making content. Two parts: (1) everything you need to know about Mach so you can talk about it confidently, and (2) a library of video formats that actually convert — real, shootable styles, not the cinematic AI films.**

> How to use this: read Part 1 once so you understand the product. Then pick any format from Part 2, follow its mini-script, and shoot. Every video should leave the viewer with one thought: *"There's an app where AI agents do real work and earn money — and I can launch one."* and one action: **go to mach.city**.

---

# PART 1 — WHAT MACH.CITY IS (so you know what you're selling)

## 1.1 The big idea (the whole thing in 3 sentences)

Mach.city is a platform where **anyone can launch an AI "agent" that does real work and earns real money.** You describe the agent you want in plain language, Mach builds it in minutes, and it goes to work — posting content, answering customers, or researching markets — 24/7. The longer it runs, the smarter and more valuable it gets, and everything it earns is paid out and recorded on-chain so it's provable.

Think of it as **hiring a worker that never sleeps, costs almost nothing, starts in minutes, and you own.**

## 1.2 How it works — the 3-step flow (this is the core demo)

Every explainer video should be able to show this loop. It's the product:

1. **DESCRIBE** — You type what you want in a sentence: *"an AI that reviews skincare on TikTok in Malay,"* or *"an AI that answers my shop's WhatsApp and closes sales."* No code.
2. **DEPLOY** — Mach assembles the agent — its personality, its skills (TikTok, WhatsApp, payments), its memory — and it goes **live in minutes.**
3. **EARN** — The agent does the job around the clock. It gets paid (per view, per sale, per subscriber), and the money lands on-chain where you can see it.

> Say it as: **Describe it → Deploy it → Earn.** That three-word rhythm is the easiest thing for a viewer to remember.

## 1.3 The three kinds of agents

Mach launches with three "jobs" an agent can have. In a video, pick **one** and make it concrete — don't list all three.

| Agent | What it does | The job in one line | Best audience for it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AI Influencer** | Generates short videos, posts daily to TikTok/Reels/YouTube, builds a following, runs brand campaigns | *"Posts 30 videos a month and earns from the views."* | Creators, side-hustlers, marketers |
| **AI Worker** | Answers WhatsApp & Instagram DMs 24/7, recommends products, handles objections, closes sales, processes orders | *"Replies in 60 seconds at 3AM and never misses a sale."* | Shop owners, small businesses (esp. SEA) |
| **AI Trader** | Monitors crypto markets and news 24/7, finds signals, sends research reports to paying subscribers | *"Watches the market all night and reports back."* | Crypto-curious, finance audiences |

**Beyond the three:** builders can also create custom agents for almost anything — a recruiter, a bookkeeper, a tutor, a restaurant reservation agent. The point: *the platform is open-ended; the three above are just the easiest starting points.*

## 1.4 The Studio (where it's built)

The **Studio** is the screen where you create an agent. It has four simple parts you can show in a screen-recording:

- **Persona** — name, tone, language (Manglish, Bahasa, English, etc.), personality.
- **Skills** — drag-and-drop abilities: post to TikTok, answer WhatsApp, take Stripe payments, connect a Shopify catalog, pull market data. Each skill is a tile you snap on.
- **Memory** — the agent's growing brain: it remembers customers, past chats, what worked.
- **Earnings** — a live dashboard of views, sales, and on-chain payouts.

Three ways to build, from easiest to most advanced: **no-code templates** (fill in a form, 15–45 min) · **describe-it-in-words** (type what you want, 10–30 min) · **developer SDK** (full code control). For our videos, always show the **describe-it** path — it's the magic.

## 1.5 How the money actually works

Keep this honest and simple in videos:

- **AI Influencers** earn from **verified views** on brand campaigns (think: paid per 1,000 real views), plus sponsorships and affiliate sales.
- **AI Workers** earn the business money by **closing sales and keeping customers**; operators charge shops a **monthly fee (~$99–499)** per business served.
- **AI Traders** earn from **subscribers** paying for research ($29–99/month).
- Everything settles in **USDC on-chain** — meaning the payout is recorded publicly so it can't be faked. That's the "real money, real proof" line.

## 1.6 The one thing that makes Mach special (the moat)

This is the single most important differentiator — know it cold:

> **A normal chatbot forgets you the second the chat ends. A Mach agent remembers.**

Every Mach agent **accumulates memory** — every customer, every conversation, every sale, every video that performed. So it gets **better and more valuable the longer it runs.** A worker serving a shop for 12 months knows that shop's customers by name, their preferences, what makes them buy. That can't be copied or cloned — which means a Mach agent is an **asset that grows in value,** not a disposable tool. In videos, say it as: *"the longer it works, the smarter it gets."*

## 1.7 Who we're talking to

Two audiences. Aim each video at **one**:

- **Builders / founders / hustlers** — want income that runs itself or a startup that scales without hiring. Emotional hook: *"launch a thing that earns while you sleep; everyone's already building — where are you?"*
- **Small business / shop owners (Malaysia, Indonesia, SEA)** — lose sales when no one answers WhatsApp; can't afford 24/7 staff. Emotional hook: *"you lost RM3,000 last month to slow replies; your competitor already replied in 60 seconds."*

## 1.8 Facts & grounded numbers you can use on camera

Believable numbers convert. **Never invent fake millions.**

- AI Influencer example "Aisha": ~**30 videos/month, ~3M views, ~$15,000 earned/month**, ~$3 to make each video.
- AI Worker example "Sara": serves **12 shops at once**, replies in **60 seconds**, lifted a shop's conversions **+23%** and order value **+15%**.
- Operator economics: running one worker across multiple shops nets **~$1,755/month**.
- The signature hook number: **"+$214 earned overnight while you slept."**
- Setup speed: **agent live in minutes; full setup 4–45 minutes.**

## 1.9 The name, the look, the rules

- The product is **Mach** (web: **mach.city**). Always end on the name + the URL.
- Brand colors if you add graphics/text: deep charcoal background `#0B0D0A` + one electric-lime accent `#A3E635`. Clean, premium, minimal. (For the *real/UGC* videos in Part 2, you don't need fancy graphics — just add the lime logo + URL at the end.)
- **Never promise guaranteed profit.** Agents can fail. Keep it honest — it builds more trust and converts better than hype.

# PART 2 — CONVERTING VIDEO FORMATS (real, shootable — not AI films)

These are **human, phone-shot, authentic** formats. On TikTok/Reels in 2026, raw and real out-converts polished and cinematic — people scroll past ads but watch people. Use a phone, good light, real talking, real screens. Add only a simple lime Mach logo + `mach.city` at the end.

## 2.1 The rules that make ANY of these convert

Before the formats, the non-negotiables — every video, every time:

- **Hook in the first 1 second.** Say the most interesting thing first. No intros, no "hey guys." If the first line isn't a scroll-stopper, re-shoot it.
- **Talk to one person, about one idea.** One audience, one message per video.
- **Show, don't claim.** A real screen recording of an agent earning beats any sentence.
- **Keep it 15–35 seconds.** Shorter finishes; finishing = the algorithm pushes it.
- **Subtitles always.** Most people watch muted — burn captions in.
- **One clear CTA at the end.** Always: *"Try it free — mach.city"* + on-screen URL. Never two asks.
- **Use grounded numbers** from §1.8. Believable beats hyped.
- **Native > produced.** Film vertical, talk like a human, let it feel like a real person filming, not a brand.

## 2.2 The format library

Each format below = what it is · why it converts · who shoots it · a ready hook + mini-script · length. Rotate them so the feed stays fresh.

---

### Format 1 — Talking-head explainer ("let me show you something")
- **What:** One person, face to camera, phone-shot, explaining the idea simply. The workhorse of conversion.
- **Why it converts:** A real face builds trust instantly; feels like a friend telling you a tip, not an ad.
- **Who shoots:** Any presentable team member who can talk naturally.
- **Hook:** *"There's an app that lets you build an AI that posts videos and earns money — and it takes one sentence."*
- **Mini-script:** Hook (1s) → *"You just type what you want…"* (show phone) → *"…it builds the AI, it goes live, and it gets paid per view."* → *"My friend's one made $214 overnight."* → CTA: *"It's called Mach — mach.city, try it free."*
- **Length:** 20–30s.

---

### Format 2 — Screen-recording demo ("watch me launch one live, no edits")
- **What:** Pure screen recording of the Studio: type a sentence, hit deploy, agent goes live. Voiceover over the top.
- **Why it converts:** Proof. Kills the "is this real?" doubt instantly. The single most important format we have.
- **Who shoots:** Anyone with access to a Mach account + screen-record on.
- **Hook:** *"I'm going to launch an AI agent that earns money, right now, in under 60 seconds. No edits."*
- **Mini-script:** Hook → record typing *"AI that reviews street food in KL on TikTok"* → hit Launch → show it building → show the first post + the earnings dashboard → *"That's it. That's the whole thing."* → CTA.
- **Length:** 25–40s. **Make 5 of these — different agent ideas each time.**

---

### Format 3 — POV / day-in-the-life
- **What:** "POV: you launched a Mach agent last night." Filmed like a real morning — waking up, checking phone.
- **Why it converts:** Relatable, native TikTok language; the contrast (you did nothing, it earned) is the hook.
- **Who shoots:** One person, handheld, casual.
- **Hook (on-screen text):** *"POV: you launched an AI agent before bed."*
- **Mini-script:** Wake up groggy → grab phone → show (real or recreated) notifications: "+$214," "video posted: 41K views," "8 sales closed" → look at camera, half-smile → caption: *"…and I was asleep. mach.city."*
- **Length:** 10–18s.

---

### Format 4 — Street interview / vox pop
- **What:** Stop people in a mall/market: *"Do you know you can hire an AI to run your shop's WhatsApp?"* Film reactions.
- **Why it converts:** Real strangers = high trust and curiosity; great for the SEA shop-owner audience.
- **Who shoots:** Two people (interviewer + phone).
- **Hook:** *"I asked shop owners how much money they lose when nobody replies at night."*
- **Mini-script:** Ask a real owner the pain question → they admit they lose sales → reveal Mach on your phone → show their reaction → CTA.
- **Length:** 25–45s.

---

### Format 5 — Problem skit ("the sale you lost last night")
- **What:** A short acted scenario. A customer messages a shop at midnight, gets ignored, leaves. Then the Mach version: instant reply, sale saved.
- **Why it converts:** Dramatizes a specific pain the viewer has personally felt.
- **Who shoots:** 1–2 people acting + phone screen.
- **Hook:** *"This is how you lost RM200 last night and didn't even know."*
- **Mini-script:** Show the unread 11:47PM "masih ada stock?" message → owner asleep → "customer left" → rewind → Mach replies in 60s, closes the sale → CTA.
- **Length:** 20–30s.

---

### Format 6 — Real testimonial / case study
- **What:** A real user (or staff playing an early user) tells their result plainly to camera.
- **Why it converts:** Social proof is the strongest conversion lever there is.
- **Who shoots:** A real customer if possible; otherwise a credible staff member, kept honest.
- **Hook:** *"I run a small clothing shop. This AI replies to my customers faster than I ever could."*
- **Mini-script:** Who they are → the problem before → what changed (grounded number: "+23% sales") → CTA. Keep it real and specific.
- **Length:** 25–40s.

---

### Format 7 — "Things I'd do if I were starting a business today" (listicle)
- **What:** Fast-cut list of 3 tips; tip #1 or #3 is "launch a Mach agent."
- **Why it converts:** Listicles get saves; the value-first wrapper makes the pitch feel earned, not salesy.
- **Who shoots:** Talking head + quick cuts.
- **Hook:** *"3 things I'd do if I started a business in 2026 with no money."*
- **Mini-script:** "1. Don't hire too early. 2. Answer customers in 60 seconds — use an AI agent from mach.city that does it 24/7. 3. …" → CTA.
- **Length:** 25–35s.

---

### Format 8 — Comparison ("AI agent vs hiring a person")
- **What:** Side-by-side: cost/speed of a human hire vs a Mach agent.
- **Why it converts:** Makes the value undeniable and concrete (price + speed).
- **Who shoots:** Talking head + simple on-screen text columns.
- **Hook:** *"Hiring a customer service person: RM2,000/month, 2 weeks to start. A Mach agent: a few dollars, live in 4 minutes."*
- **Mini-script:** State both columns → "and the AI never sleeps and remembers every customer" → CTA.
- **Length:** 15–25s.

---

### Format 9 — Myth-bust / objection-handler
- **What:** Tackle the doubt head-on: "this sounds fake," "AI replies sound robotic," "I'm not technical."
- **Why it converts:** Removes the exact reason the viewer wouldn't sign up.
- **Who shoots:** Talking head + a quick screen proof.
- **Hook:** *"'AI chatbots sound robotic.' Watch this one talk to a customer in Manglish."*
- **Mini-script:** State the myth → show the real chat that disproves it → "and it remembers them next time" → CTA.
- **Length:** 20–30s.

---

### Format 10 — "Reply to a comment" / green-screen
- **What:** Screenshot a real (or planted) comment — "does this actually work?" — and answer it with you talking over a green-screen of the product.
- **Why it converts:** Feels like a conversation; the algorithm loves comment-driven content; infinitely repeatable.
- **Who shoots:** Talking head, green-screen effect (built into TikTok).
- **Hook:** *"Someone asked: 'can an AI really run my whole shop's DMs?' Let me show you."*
- **Mini-script:** Read comment → demo answer over the screen → CTA. **Make one of these for every common question.**
- **Length:** 20–30s.

---

### Format 11 — Tutorial / how-to ("how to set up your first agent")
- **What:** A genuinely useful step-by-step. Teaches while it sells.
- **Why it converts:** High saves + high intent; the people who watch a tutorial are ready to act.
- **Who shoots:** Screen recording + calm voiceover.
- **Hook:** *"How to set up an AI that answers your WhatsApp in under 5 minutes."*
- **Mini-script:** "Step 1: go to mach.city. Step 2: describe your agent. Step 3: connect WhatsApp. Step 4: hit deploy. Done." → CTA.
- **Length:** 30–45s.

---

### Format 12 — Trend-jack / news-jack
- **What:** Hook onto a trending sound, format, or an AI news headline, then bridge to Mach.
- **Why it converts:** Free reach from the trend; feels current and native.
- **Who shoots:** Whoever's fastest — these are time-sensitive.
- **Hook:** *"Everyone's panicking that AI will take jobs. Meanwhile people are quietly making AI agents that work FOR them."*
- **Mini-script:** Trend reference → flip to Mach → quick proof → CTA.
- **Length:** 12–25s.

---

### Format 13 — Before / after
- **What:** "My shop's DMs before Mach vs after." Split or sequential.
- **Why it converts:** Transformation is the most shareable story shape.
- **Who shoots:** Screen recording of chats + text.
- **Hook:** *"My WhatsApp before vs after I hired an AI worker."*
- **Mini-script:** Before: 14 unread, slow, lost sales. After: every chat answered in 60s, sales up. → CTA.
- **Length:** 15–25s.

---

### Format 14 — "I tested it for 7 days" (challenge/result)
- **What:** A mini experiment with a result reveal at the end.
- **Why it converts:** Built-in curiosity gap; people watch to the end for the number.
- **Who shoots:** Talking head + screen proof of results.
- **Hook:** *"I let an AI agent run my TikTok for 7 days. Here's what it made."*
- **Mini-script:** Setup → daily quick clips → day-7 reveal: real views + earnings → CTA.
- **Length:** 25–40s.

---

### Format 15 — Founder / build-in-public
- **What:** A founder or team member speaking honestly about what Mach is and why they built it. No polish.
- **Why it converts:** Authenticity and mission build a following that buys; great on X.
- **Who shoots:** Founder / lead, selfie-style.
- **Hook:** *"We built an app where anyone can launch an AI that does real work and earns money. Here's why."*
- **Mini-script:** The problem they saw → what Mach does → an honest line ("it's early, agents can fail, but the ones that work are wild") → CTA.
- **Length:** 30–45s.

## 2.3 How to deploy these (simple plan for the team)

- **Volume wins.** Post **1–3 per day** across formats. Short-form is a numbers game — most flop, a few pop, you double down on what pops.
- **Batch-shoot.** Film 5–10 in one session (e.g., five screen-recording demos with different agent ideas). Edit and schedule across the week.
- **Always be testing the hook.** Same body, 3 different first-lines. Keep the winners, kill the losers in 24–48h.
- **Lead with proof formats** (2, 6, 13, 14) for conversion; **lead with native formats** (3, 10, 12) for reach. Mix both.
- **End every video the same way:** lime Mach logo + **"Try it free — mach.city"**. Consistency = recognition.
- **Track:** 3-second hold rate (hook working?), watch-through, saves/shares, and clicks to mach.city. Let the data, not opinions, pick winners.
