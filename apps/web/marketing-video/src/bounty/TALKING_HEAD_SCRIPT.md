# Talking-head version — Malaysia's unpaid palm-oil bill

Target: 70–80 seconds, English, 9:16. Speak naturally, not like a voice-over. Green-screen images support *your* explanation; your face stays visible through most of the video. This is a proposed market design, not an existing investment product.

## Teleprompter script

If I could tokenize one thing in Malaysia, it wouldn't be a palm tree.

It would be the bill a palm-oil mill still needs to pay.

Here's what I mean. Small farmers harvest fresh fruit bunches. A cooperative takes them to a mill. The mill weighs the fruit, checks its quality, agrees on a price, and owes the cooperative money.

Malaysia's independent smallholders farm over 818,000 hectares. But growing and moving that fruit takes cash: fertilizer, workers, transport.

So here's my idea. **Only after the mill accepts the delivery**, turn that confirmed payment claim into small digital pieces on Solana. Approved investors could fund those pieces. The cooperative gets money sooner and can pay farmers sooner. When the mill pays, the investors are repaid.

Why Solana? It can keep one clear record of who owns each piece, automate payouts, and restrict transfers to approved investors.

But a token cannot prove the weighbridge was honest. And it cannot guarantee the mill will pay. This still needs audits, enforceable contracts, and compliance with Malaysia's securities rules.

So I wouldn't tokenize the tree. **I'd tokenize the payment after its fruit is sold.**

## Style and filming

- Film vertical 4K/30fps if possible. Frame chest-up, eyes around the top third, body a little left of center to leave space for visuals on the right. Keep the safe area clear of platform UI at the very top and bottom.
- Use a lavalier or close microphone. Clear audio matters more than 4K. Speak at roughly 145–155 words per minute; take deliberate pauses at the hook, “only after,” and the close.
- Light the green screen evenly and stand at least 1.5 m from it to avoid green spill. Use a soft key light on your face, with clothes that are not green.
- Cuts should be quick but not frantic: photo background, two simple diagrams, mock receipt, clean close. No spinning coins, price charts, or fake “verified” documents.
- Keep captions in sentence case, two lines maximum. Highlight just one word at a time: **bill**, **accepted**, **Solana**, **audit**. Do not cover your mouth or the evidence graphic.
- Use the existing original palm-harvest illustration at `public/bounty/palm-harvest.png` as atmosphere only; label the receipt *illustrative mock-up*. A real cooperative/mill partner's approved footage would be stronger if you can obtain it.

## Why these claims are safe

- [MPOB 2024 planted-area data](https://bepi.mpob.gov.my/images/area/2024/Area_summary2024.pdf) gives independent smallholders 818,678 hectares. Say “over 818,000 hectares” and show the 2024 year.
- [MPOB on smallholder cooperatives](https://mpob.gov.my/2026/01/sustainable-oil-palm-growers-cooperatives-empower-independent-oil-palm-smallholders-economies/) confirms that cooperatives can sell fresh fruit bunches directly to mills and operate weighing centres.
- [MPOB pricing guidance](https://bepi.mpob.gov.my/index.php/import/1015-notification-on-mpob-daily-fresh-fruit-bunches-ffb-reference-price-at-1-oer) says its published FFB figure is a reference, not the price fixed for every trade.
- [Solana tokenization documentation](https://solana.com/docs/tokenization) describes on-chain issuance, settlement and token compliance controls. The concept does not require claiming that Solana can validate real-world weights by itself.
- [Securities Commission Malaysia](https://www.sc.com.my/resources/media/media-release/sc-seeks-public-feedback-on-proposed-framework-on-tokenisation-of-capital-market-products) says tokenized capital-market products remain subject to securities regulation. Have a Malaysian lawyer assess claim assignment and offering structure before presenting this as live.
