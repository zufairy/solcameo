# Tokenize the receipt, not the palm tree

English 9:16 short-form video for Superteam's “Explain Market Tokenization in Your Country” bounty. Country: Malaysia. Target: 66 seconds. This is a proposed design, not a live investable product or promise of returns.

## Voice-over (record this verbatim if replacing the draft voice)

Malaysia's palm oil story has a missing asset. Not the land. Not the fruit. The payment owed after a smallholder's harvest is accepted.

In 2024, independent smallholders cultivated over eight hundred and eighteen thousand hectares. Today, fresh fruit bunches move from farm to dealer or mill. Weight and quality shape the sale. The Malaysian Palm Oil Board publishes a reference price, but buyer and seller agree the actual price.

Meanwhile, growers need cash for inputs, labour, transport and the next harvest.

Imagine a cooperative's mill-accepted delivery receipt becoming a verified, short-dated payment claim. Pool the claims. Issue small units to eligible investors on a compliant ledger. Growers can get an advance; investors are repaid when the buyer settles. A licensed venue might allow resale, but cannot guarantee liquidity.

But tokenization does not fix a dishonest weighbridge or a buyer who defaults. Audits, credit checks, enforceable assignment and securities rules come first.

Don't tokenize the palm tree. Tokenize the proof it was sold.

## Sources and claim checks

- [MPOB, Oil Palm Planted Area 2024](https://bepi.mpob.gov.my/images/area/2024/Area_summary2024.pdf): independent smallholders 818,678 hectares, 14.6% of total planted area. On-screen number is historical, not a current 2026 estimate.
- [MPOB, Daily FFB Reference Price at 1% OER](https://bepi.mpob.gov.my/index.php/import/1015-notification-on-mpob-daily-fresh-fruit-bunches-ffb-reference-price-at-1-oer): the reference is a benchmark, and MPOB does not determine each sale price; OER/quality matters.
- [MPOB Smallholder Empowerment Unit](https://mpob.gov.my/2025/08/smallholder-empowerment-unit/): co-operatives market FFB and government programs finance agricultural inputs. Supports the real financing need without inventing typical payment terms.
- [Securities Commission Malaysia, May 2025 consultation](https://www.sc.com.my/resources/media/media-release/sc-seeks-public-feedback-on-proposed-framework-on-tokenisation-of-capital-market-products): describes fractional ownership, record-keeping, and the principle that tokenized capital-market products remain subject to securities regulation. Do not present the proposed framework as a blanket approval for this product.
- [SC Malaysia, 2026 Capital Market Masterplan](https://www.sc.com.my/api/documentms/download.ashx?id=5465cb2e-48de-40c7-b05f-9c3f5fc959c8): describes tokenized securities mechanics and investor safeguards. This film's receivables structure would still need Malaysian legal and regulatory analysis.
- [Superteam bounty](https://superteam.fun/earn/listing/explain-market-tokenization-in-your-country): English short-form video is an accepted submission format. Check the live deadline before posting.
