## 0. Read this first — how to use this document

Each of the 30 videos is a self-contained brief with seven fields:

1. **Hook (0–2s)** — the first frame and first line. If this fails, nothing else matters.
2. **Format / length / audience** — 9:16 or 16:9, run time, who it's for.
3. **Scene breakdown** — each shot, the camera angle, and the **Seedance prompt** to paste.
4. **On-screen text** — exact words, timed to the shot.
5. **Audio** — music feel, SFX, and voiceover (VO) lines if any.
6. **CTA** — the closing frame and the caption's call to action.
7. **Why it works** — the psychological lever (FOMO, status, pain, proof, curiosity).

Produce them in any order. Videos are grouped into **6 series** so you can batch-shoot a look in one sitting and keep the feed varied.

## 1. What Mach is (the one-paragraph version every video must serve)

Mach is a protocol where anyone can **launch an autonomous AI agent that does real work and gets paid** — an AI influencer that posts and runs brand campaigns, an AI worker that answers WhatsApp and closes sales 24/7, an AI trader that researches markets. You describe the agent in plain language; it goes to work in minutes; it earns; and because it **remembers every customer and every win, it gets more valuable the longer it runs** — something a generic chatbot can never do. Builders launch and own agents. Businesses rent them. The whole thing settles on-chain.

**The two stories we tell in this batch:**

- **To builders:** *"You can launch a thing that earns money while you sleep — today, in minutes. Everyone else is already building. Where are you?"*
- **To SMB owners:** *"Every night a customer messages your shop and nobody answers, you lose the sale. Mach's worker never sleeps, speaks your customer's language, and remembers them."*

## 2. The brand world — make it look big and premium

Every video must feel like it came from a category-defining company, not a side project. The look is locked, pulled from the product's real design system (`DESIGN.md` — "Lime Workshop"). Consistency is what makes a small company look enormous.

**Visual signature (paste the relevant pieces into every Seedance prompt):**

- **Canvas:** deep machined charcoal `#0B0D0A` — near-black, faintly green-warm. **Never pure black, never white-background.**
- **The one accent:** electric **lime `#A3E635`** — used like a single signal flare. One lime element per frame: a glowing core, a line of light, a key number, the CTA. Discipline here is the whole premium feel.
- **Surfaces:** "3D plated" — physical machined metal tiles with real depth, inset top highlight, a solid dark ledge underneath. Think precision workshop at night, not glossy glass.
- **Type on screen:** `Space Grotesk` for headlines (tight, confident, lime on a single word only), `Geist Mono` for stats, labels, timestamps.
- **Motion:** cinematic and calm — slow push-ins, orbiting cores, self-drawing light, parallax depth. Kinetic energy lives in the *illustration*, so the copy can stay short.
- **Banned (kills the premium feel instantly):** emojis on screen, neon outer-glow, gradient-filled big headers, a second accent color, generic stock-3D, fake round numbers, "scroll to explore" arrows.

**The reusable Seedance style string** (append to almost every prompt):

> `Cinematic, high-end product-film look. Deep charcoal #0B0D0A environment, faintly green-warm, never pure black. Single electric-lime #A3E635 accent as the only bright color, used sparingly like a signal light. Machined metal plated surfaces with real depth and soft inset highlights, precision-workshop-at-night mood. Volumetric haze, shallow depth of field, slow deliberate camera, anamorphic, 35mm, ultra-detailed, photoreal, 24fps motion-blur, color-graded premium tech ad.`

**The angle/shot library** (rotate these so 30 videos never feel same-y — "many angles, memorable"):

- Slow dolly **push-in** on a single glowing agent core (hero / authority).
- **Low-angle hero shot** looking up at a monolith / server tower (makes Mach feel huge).
- **Top-down (god view)** of a swarm of agents lighting up a dark map (scale).
- **Macro** on a single plated tile as text etches into the metal (craft / premium).
- **Over-the-shoulder phone frame** — a real WhatsApp/TikTok UI lighting up (relatable).
- **Whip-pan / flash-cut montage** between agent "characters" (energy / FOMO).
- **Orbit** around a floating UI panel in 3D space (product as artifact).
- **First-person walk** through an infinite dark hall of glowing agent lockers (world-building).

## 3. The FOMO engine — the levers each video pulls

Memorable ads don't list features; they create a feeling the viewer can't shake. Across the 30 we deliberately rotate six levers so the feed hits from every side:

- **Status / envy** — "the builders who get this are already eating." Show winners, imply you're late.
- **Pain agitation** — "you lost RM2,000 last night and didn't even know." Make the wound specific.
- **Effortless power** — one sentence in, a working money-making agent out. The magic gap.
- **Proof / receipts** — on-chain payouts, ticking counters, real grounded numbers (+23% conversion, $1,755/mo net — never fake millions).
- **Scale / inevitability** — swarms, maps lighting up, "an economy, not an app." We look unstoppable.
- **Curiosity / pattern-break** — a weird cold open that makes the thumb stop, then pays it off.

## 4. Production playbook — how to actually shoot these in Seedance

### 4.1 The two reusable prompt blocks (copy once, reuse everywhere)

**STYLE BLOCK** (append to every prompt — this is what makes 30 videos feel like one brand):

> `Cinematic, high-end product-film look. Deep charcoal #0B0D0A environment, faintly green-warm, never pure black. Single electric-lime #A3E635 accent as the only bright color, used sparingly like a signal light. Machined metal plated surfaces with real depth and soft inset highlights, precision-workshop-at-night mood. Volumetric haze, shallow depth of field, slow deliberate camera, anamorphic, 35mm, ultra-detailed, photoreal, premium tech ad, no text artifacts, no watermark.`

**NEGATIVE BLOCK** (paste into Seedance's negative prompt field if available):

> `pure black background, pure white background, neon outer glow, rainbow colors, multiple bright colors, cartoonish, low quality, blurry, distorted faces, extra fingers, watermark, stock-photo look, cluttered, busy, ugly UI, gradient headlines, emojis, text gibberish.`

### 4.2 Aspect ratio & length

- Generate **9:16** clips for TikTok / Reels / Shorts; **16:9** for X / YouTube / the website hero.
- Seedance clips are short — produce each scene as its **own 4–8s generation**, then cut them together. Don't try to prompt a whole 20s film in one go; you lose control of the angle.
- For a 16:9 hero you also want a 9:16 cut — re-frame in edit or use the `reframe` tool, don't re-shoot.

### 4.3 Keeping characters consistent (Aisha, Sara, the agent cores)

Seedance drifts between generations. To keep recurring "characters" recognizable across videos:

1. Generate **one strong reference still** of each character first (lock the look you like).
2. Use **image-to-video** from that still for every subsequent shot of that character, rather than text-to-video from scratch.
3. Keep a one-line "character bible" in the prompt every time (e.g., *"Aisha: 22, warm brown skin, glossy dark hair, lime circuit shimmer under skin, confident"*).
4. Save approved stills in `public/gen/` next to the existing renders so the whole team pulls from the same source.

### 4.4 On-screen text & logo — add in edit, not in Seedance

AI video still mangles text. **Generate clean footage with no words baked in**, then add all on-screen text, the MACH wordmark, and stat cards in your editor (CapCut / Premiere / After Effects, or the existing Remotion pipeline in this folder — `npm run render`). Use Space Grotesk + Geist Mono to match the brand. This also keeps text crisp and lets you localize (Manglish/Bahasa) without re-generating.

### 4.5 Audio

Seedance output is silent or unreliable for sound. Score in edit: license one or two premium tech/cinematic tracks and reuse them across the batch (sonic branding). Add SFX (keystrokes, the "PAID" cha-ching, notification chimes) from a library. Keep everything **autoplay-muted-safe** — every video must work with the sound off, which is why all copy is on-screen.

### 4.6 The angle/shot library (rotate so nothing repeats)

Pull from these eight so the 30 never blur together: macro push-in on a single core · low-angle hero monolith · top-down god-view swarm · macro on a plated tile etching · over-the-shoulder phone UI · whip-pan character montage · 3D orbit around a UI panel · first-person walk through the agent hall.
