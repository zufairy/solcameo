# Vello UGC Campaign — 5-Account Series Plan

Five X accounts, five *distinct recurring series*. Each is a character with a spine, not a random posting schedule — so the audience comes back for the story, and Vello rides inside it. Every series sells **the paycheck, not the robot**, uses **exact numbers**, and ends on **vello.gg — free month, no card**.

**Before anything ships — the one hard rule:** every payout dashboard, view count, and earnings screenshot must come from a **real Vello agent actually running on that account**. In the clipping niche, one faked receipt is permanent death on the exact channels we grow on. So Day 0 for all five = mint a real agent, enroll it in a live bounty, and let it earn. The series then *documents what actually happened*. No invented numbers, ever.

Assigned segments (so the five don't cannibalize each other):

| Account | Character | Series | Format | Funnel job |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| @clipsbymarcus | Clipper who automated his night shift | **The Night Shift** | 30-day doc experiment, receipts | Convert individual clippers |
| @haileystacey | Chaotic creator + her AI "intern" | **Me & My Agent** | Skit / drama, recurring characters (generated) | Top-funnel reach + relatability |
| @balaji_krishnan | Operator building a one-man agency | **Agent Ops** | Build-in-public, screen-record dashboards | Convert agencies / operators |
| @cryptomatt | Degen KOL, on-chain receipts | **Proof of Post** | Fast-cut alpha, USDC payout screenshots | Trust + web3 founders/KOLs |
| @anny_nyc | NYC one-woman media empire | **One-Woman Agency** | Lifestyle vlog + generated brainrot skit inserts | Aspiration / awareness |

Rotate formats *within* each account too — never the same beat two days running. Judge every video at day 3–5, not day 1.

## 1. @clipsbymarcus — "The Night Shift"

**The character:** a real clipper who's tired of the 2am grind — reads briefs, cuts, submits by hand, misses windows. Day 1 he hands his night shift to a Vello agent and films what happens. This is the **N-day experiment with receipts** — the single highest-converting format in this niche.

**Format:** faceless-friendly. Screen recordings of the agent running + dashboard, tight voiceover, one talking-head cold open per week. Vertical, 20–40s. The payout dashboard is the recurring visual signature.

**30-day beat sheet:**

- **Days 1–3 — The setup.** "I clip on Whop. Last month I missed a $300 payout by 4 minutes. Day 1 of letting an agent take my night shift." Show the mint (15 min), first bounty enrolled. Hook = the confession.
- **Days 4–10 — First receipts.** Every 2–3 days: "Night 3. It posted 11 clips while I slept. Here's the dashboard." Real numbers only — whatever the agent actually did.
- **Days 11–20 — The scale.** "I stopped checking it. Week 2: [real] clips, [real] verified views." Introduce the rate: "$1–3 per 1,000 verified views, and I did none of the cutting."
- **Days 21–27 — The doubt beat.** Show a clip that flopped or a bounty that capped out — honesty *is* the proof format. "Not every clip hits. Here's what the agent killed and what it kept."
- **Days 28–30 — The payout reveal.** The money shot: 30-day earnings dashboard + first cash-out. "30 days, one agent, [real $] paid out. I clipped none of it. Your turn — vello.gg, free month."

**Sample hooks (rotate):**
1. "I missed a $300 payout by 4 minutes. So I fired myself from the night shift."
2. "Night 6: it posted 14 clips. I was asleep for all of them."
3. "Stop clipping manually. You're competing against software now."
4. "The dashboard after 2 weeks of an agent doing my night shift 👇"
5. "I let one agent run my Whop clipping for 30 days. Full receipts."

**Cadence:** 1 post/day, 2 on payout-reveal days. **CTA:** "Same setup, free 30 days, no card — vello.gg" (+ affiliate link).

**Cadence:** 1 skit/day, keep the through-line. **CTA (soft, top-funnel):** "hire your own intern free for 30 days — vello.gg."

## Shared production rules (all 5 accounts)

**Real receipts or nothing.** Mint a real agent on each account Day 0, enroll in a live bounty, and only ever show numbers that agent actually produced. Every series is designed so the *story* carries even in a slow earnings week — but the numbers on screen are always real.

**Language guardrails (banned everywhere):** "passive income," "make money while you sleep" (as a promise), "get rich," "bot," "AI employee," "digital worker," "content farm/UGC farm," "mass-post," "spam," "AI-generated content" as a self-label, "replace your team." Preferred: agent, clipper, get paid to post, cash out, verified views, engine, distribution. State autonomy as receipts ("posted 47 clips overnight"), never as a promise.

**Every hook carries an exact number** — a rate ($1–3 / 1,000 verified views), a count ("posted 40× this week"), or a time-collapse ("mint in 15 minutes").

**Cross-account crossovers (Week 3+):** have them react to each other's payout reveals (@cryptomatt "verifying" @clipsbymarcus's dashboard on-chain; @anny_nyc "hiring" @balaji's agency setup). One shared universe = more watch-time and a believable ecosystem.

**Cadence & CTA:** ~1 post/account/day, 2 on payout-reveal days. Link slots are for @cryptomatt and @clipsbymarcus (link posts suppress reach — the lifestyle/skit accounts keep vello.gg in bio + comments instead). CTA everywhere: **free 30 days, no card, cancel in one click — vello.gg** (+ 40% recurring affiliate link once each account has earned real receipts).

**Tracking table to keep (per account, per week):**

| Account | Series | Videos posted | Best hook | Views | New follows | vello.gg clicks | Mints attributed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Judge each video at day 3–5. Kill formats that don't move follows or clicks; double the ones that do.
