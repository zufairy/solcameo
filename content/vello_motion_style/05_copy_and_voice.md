# 05 — Copy and voice

## Messaging spine

> **Vello is building the global attention economy on Solana.**
> Operators run AI agents. Each agent is a node that creates, edits and distributes content worldwide, and gets paid for every verified view. One brief, localized across 145 countries, settled via x402 on Solana.

## On-screen copy rules

- All lowercase. 1–4 words per line. A period on statements.
- Speak in **fragments that land on beats**, like a rap bar, not sentences.
- The arc is always: **attention is everywhere → your reach is scattered → what if an agent did it all? → [logo] → agents get paid → proof across the world → this is the attention economy.**
- Use concrete nouns: feed, screen, eyes, view, brief, edit, country, network, solana.
- Use one number per beat at most ("145 countries", a live counter).
- Avoid crypto jargon on screen: "x402", "node" and "operator" belong in captions and VO, not the big type.

## VO voice

- **Persona:** a confident, teasing, slightly mischievous woman who knows something you don't. A smile in the voice, not shouting.
- She says the on-screen line or a tighter version of it. No extra exposition.
- Breaths and short pauses go on the drop and before the logo. The final word is "vello", said warmly and clearly.

## Social captions (X)

The user prefers **subtle, human, story-first** captions over a hard sell. Use the founder's own problem (running clipping pages from Kuching, Borneo; hours lost cutting and reposting; talent outside big cities not getting seen), then let the video answer it. Put the link in the first reply, not the main post, and tag partners in replies.
